PARTS 5–7 · PRODUCTION, DISTRIBUTION, RESOURCES

Four weeks, five channels, zero ad money.

The four weeks
WEEK 0Get ready. Free short address. Export the posts. Ask 5 classmates to try it first and send one challenge link each.
WEEK 1Reveal. Key post in the class groups, then one story a day: Clear it → Learn it → Earn it.
WEEK 2Exam drops. Every new quiz review = one “review is out” post + WhatsApp text in that subject’s group.
WEEK 3Challenge week. Post your own result card on status. Each friend who plays sees the app with no sign-in.
WEEK 4Squads before the midterm. “Make a squad of 4.” Put the A4 poster up where you are allowed to.
Why this order: trust first (a classmate shows it), then usefulness at the moment of need (exam drops), then the loops that spread by themselves (challenges, squads).
Where students are
1 · WhatsApp class groups — the main channel. Short text + one picture. Post once; never spam.
2 · WhatsApp status / Instagram stories — result cards and the 3 stories (1080×1920).
3 · Facebook student groups — the key post and exam drops. Ask the admins first.
4 · Telegram subject channels — exam drops only.
5 · Campus walls — A4 poster with QR, only where posters are allowed.
Count it for free: new accounts in Admin → Users each week; how many friends reply to challenge links; a one-line “how did you hear about Clearn?” asked in person.

The $200 of Claude usage
What I could check: nothing about your balance — this session cannot see billing. My assumption: the credits pay for Claude’s own work (sessions like this one, Claude Design canvases, code). Check it in your Claude settings → Usage.
Not covered, not used: other tools’ own credits (image/video generators, slide makers), domains, ads. The project rule is zero spend, so I used none of them.
